The strategy is the direction the AI follows on every cycle. Five presets set it for you, from careful to all-in.
They work like the trim levels of a car: **each level does everything the level below does and adds more**. What
grows from one level to the next is how much the agent pays to grow the squad.

Pick one in **Settings → Strategy** or on Telegram (`/strategy 2 hunter`). You can edit the text afterwards or write
your own. [Every setting explained](/use/settings/) covers the field itself.

## The five levels

| Level | Adds on top of the level below | Typical spend a day |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Steady** | The free game used fully: the 5 free training sessions, lineup and formation, the 5 daily matches, sync before match days, the free team badge. Silver tournaments and 1v1s at your lowest amounts. No shop purchases, no bids | Entries and sync: a few hundred FOXSY |
| **2. Climber** | The push for ELO: ranked matches whenever allowed, Gold tournaments when the squad can compete, 1v1s at any of your amounts, condition recovery for key starters before a match that matters | Plus Gold entries (5,000 FOXSY) and some recovery |
| **3. Hunter** | Tournaments and the first purchases: creates its own tournaments, the XP booster on days with 3 sure competitive matches, paid training slots while it runs, a fusion when three spare cards are ready, bids on **Rare** players | Plus about 2,000 FOXSY on booster days and Rare bids |
| **4. Predator** | Pays for everything that grows the squad **except waivers**: all 15 training sessions every day, the booster on every match day, recovery whenever it helps, both Common packs a week, a fusion whenever a triplet is ready (real matches, full 14-day cooldown), bids on **Rare and Epic** players | About 3,500 FOXSY, plus entries and Epic bids |
| **5. Beast** | Every paid advantage: the booster always on, **both fusion waivers** so a fusion can happen every 20 hours, 3 competitive matches a day, bids on players of **any rarity, Legendaries included**; keeps 22 to 36 cards and sells the spare ones above 36 | About 6,000 FOXSY, plus entries and Legendary bids |

On average about 90 percent of all entry fees come back as prizes, so entries are mostly a stake that returns,
not a cost. The shop, the waivers and the bids are real spending.

## The limits to set for each level

The presets only set the direction. Your limits always win: an agent stops at its daily cap, its monthly budget and
its largest single payment, whatever its strategy says. Raise them with the level, or a higher level behaves like a
lower one. Rough values at a FOXSY price of about $0.0027 (October 2026):

| Level | Daily cap | Monthly budget | Max bid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steady | $10 (the default) | $300 (the default) | not used |
| Climber | $20 (a Gold entry is about $14) | $500 | not used |
| Hunter | $25 | $600 | 4,000 FOXSY |
| Predator | $40 | $1,000 | 15,000 FOXSY |
| Beast | $100 (a Legendary bid is about $95) | $2,000 | 40,000 FOXSY |

At auction a Rare started at about 2,300 FOXSY, an Epic at about 10,000 and a Legendary at about 18,000 (October
2026). A bid also needs a daily cap above its value.

## Good to know

- An agent keeps the text it has. Changing a preset here or in a later version does not change an agent's strategy
  until you pick the preset again.
- The levels describe what the AI aims for; the code still checks every action: the game's rules, your limits, a
  1v1 only against a team of similar strength, a waiver only when a fusion with that card can really happen.
- A level higher than your wallet can carry runs out of FOXSY sooner. The **Top-up warning** in Settings tells you
  when the wallet runs low.
