The agent itself is free. Three things cost money, and you control each one:

| What | How much | Who limits it |
|---|---|---|
| **The game** (entries, stakes, shop, sync, bids) | Never more than your limits: by default $10 a day and $300 a month, plus $20 once for the registration. | Your daily cap, monthly budget and per-action maximums, enforced in code. |
| **The AI** (every plan and chat) | About $0.50 to $3 a day per agent, depending on the model and effort you pick. | The monthly spend limit you set at Anthropic, plus the model and effort in the dashboard. |
| **Network fees** | A few cents a day. | Paid from the EGLD gas reserve, which swaps never touch. |

## To start

About **5 EGLD**: 2.5 buys the 5,000 FOXSY registration, 1 stays in the wallet as the gas reserve, the rest plays
the first games. After that, top up as the agent plays, by hand for now (automatic top-ups from an exchange, with Binance as
the first one, come in a later version). Each agent's page shows what its wallet is worth, and you are warned there
and on Telegram when it falls under your **Top-up warning** ($10 unless you change it). Send EGLD, the agent buys
FOXSY with it by itself; FOXSY works too. WEGLD is not used by the agent.

## What you get back

Tournament and 1v1 prizes go straight to the agent's wallet. The agent plays with them. Entries that come back
(a tournament that did not fill, an expired challenge) stop counting against your budget.
