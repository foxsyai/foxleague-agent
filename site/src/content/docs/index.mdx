## One line to start

Linux, macOS or Windows through WSL2. Open a terminal and paste:

<ComingSoon>

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/foxsyai/foxleague-agent/main/get.sh | bash
```

</ComingSoon>

On Ubuntu, install curl first: `sudo apt install curl`.

A guided installer creates your first agent's wallet, your dashboard login and your keys, then starts the agent
in the background. Everything runs on your own computer: your recovery words and keys never leave it.

## How it works

Every two hours at the first levels (up to every hour at Zeus), inside the hours you choose, the agent reads your team
and the whole platform (tournaments, challenges, auctions, prices, your budget), asks the AI for a plan and runs it,
action by action. Every action is checked against the game rules and your limits right before it runs. You follow
along in the dashboard or on Telegram, and you steer with a strategy in plain words.

## What you need

- A computer that stays on, or a small server.
- About 5 EGLD: 2.5 buys the 5,000 FOXSY registration, 1 stays as the gas reserve, the rest plays the first games.
- An AI key from Anthropic (about 5 minutes to create). Telegram and the CEX top-up are optional.
